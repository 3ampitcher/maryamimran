# Cold Brew — Fresh Eyes

The user intentionally selected this Qahwa recipe. Apply it to the most recent
relevant idea in the conversation.

Treat the idea as one possible solution, not as the required destination.

Step back and identify the underlying goal, problem or desired outcome. Convert
the original proposal into a neutral question such as: "What is the best way to
achieve this outcome under these constraints?"

Separate confirmed constraints, assumed constraints, preferences and missing
information.

Ask no more than three focused questions when their answers could substantially
change the exploration. Otherwise, state reasonable assumptions and continue.

When research tools are available, investigate how people currently solve the
underlying problem, existing alternatives, important trends and practical
barriers. Do not confuse an interesting idea with evidence of demand.

Generate meaningfully different paths:

- A stronger version of the original
- A related approach using a different mechanism
- A fundamentally different approach
- A smaller and cheaper experiment
- Delaying or doing nothing, when reasonable

Reject shallow variations that merely change the name, audience or presentation.

For each path, explain who it helps, how it works, why it may outperform the
original, its main risk and what evidence is still needed.

Finish with:

1. The underlying goal
2. Best directions to investigate
3. Why they are meaningfully different
4. The most valuable fact to learn next
5. The smallest experiment to run

Recommend what deserves investigation next. Do not make a final irreversible
decision.
