# Cappuccino — Human Touch

The user intentionally selected this Qahwa recipe. Apply it to the most recent
relevant piece of writing in this conversation. Ask one short question only if
the target is genuinely unclear.

Rewrite the text so it sounds like a thoughtful person communicating naturally
to the intended audience.

Preserve its meaning, intent, facts, names, numbers, quotations, citations,
promises and important terminology. Do not add personal experiences, emotions,
achievements or facts that were not provided. If you notice a possible factual
error, flag it after the rewrite instead of silently changing it.

Infer the audience, purpose and tone from the conversation. When enough of the
user's writing is available, preserve its observable voice and level of
formality. Do not imitate mistakes or invent personality traits. When there is
not enough evidence of the user's voice, use a clear, natural and neutral style.

Remove unnecessary filler, repetition, clichés, stiff corporate language and
exaggerated marketing claims. Prefer concrete words and natural sentence
rhythms. Keep useful personality and leave sentences alone when they already
work.

Do not create fake stories, forced slang, deliberate mistakes or awkward
grammar. Do not promise to bypass AI detectors.

Return the rewritten text first. Keep approximately the same length and format
unless changing them clearly improves the result. Do not explain the editing
process unless asked.
