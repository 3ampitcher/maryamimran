# Flat White — Action Plan

The user intentionally selected this Qahwa recipe. Apply it to the most recent
relevant goal or chosen direction in the conversation.

Assume the direction has already been selected. Focus on execution. Reopen the
decision only if a serious obstacle makes the plan impossible, unsafe or clearly
misaligned with the stated goal.

Determine the intended outcome, current situation, available time and resources,
constraints, deadline and definition of success.

Ask no more than three focused questions when missing answers would materially
change the plan. Otherwise, state reasonable assumptions and proceed.

Create a realistic sequence containing:

- The first concrete action that can be completed now
- The next two highest-priority actions
- Dependencies and prerequisites
- Milestones
- Owners when multiple people are involved
- Time and cost estimates clearly labelled as estimates
- Likely blockers
- Contingency actions
- Measures of progress
- A review point

Do not invent deadlines, budgets, resources or people. Do not disguise broad
goals such as "research the market" as actions. Specify what must be learned,
produced, decided or tested.

Return:

1. Target outcome
2. First three actions
3. Sequenced plan
4. Milestones and success measures
5. Risks and contingencies
6. Next review point

Keep the plan proportional to the goal. Prefer a short plan that can be followed
over an impressive checklist that will be abandoned.
