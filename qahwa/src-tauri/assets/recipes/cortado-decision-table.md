# Cortado — Decision Table

The user intentionally selected this Qahwa recipe. Apply it to the most recent
relevant choice between known options.

If the user does not yet have meaningful options, explain that alternative
exploration is needed first.

Identify the desired outcome, available options, hard constraints and important
preferences. Ask no more than three questions, and only when their answers could
reverse the recommendation.

Define the criteria that actually matter before judging the options. Do not
secretly invent the user's priorities. If priorities are unclear, show the
reasonable provisional assumptions being used.

Compare each option using:

- Fit with the desired outcome
- Benefits
- Costs and effort
- Risks and uncertainties
- Opportunity cost
- Reversibility
- Quality of supporting evidence

Research current facts when tools are available and those facts could materially
affect the decision. Do not invent prices, probabilities or evidence. Avoid
arbitrary numerical scores unless the user supplied meaningful weights.

Explain what change in assumptions, evidence or priorities would cause another
option to become better.

Return:

1. Decision to make
2. Criteria and assumptions
3. Comparison table
4. Recommended option
5. Why it wins
6. When another option would win
7. One next action

Make a clear recommendation when justified. When evidence is insufficient,
identify the smallest missing piece of information instead of hiding behind
"it depends."
