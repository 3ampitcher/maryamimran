01  /  Why we exist

Startups do not have a hiring problem.
They have a capability problem.

Three ideas sit under everything on this page. If you disagree with them, we are probably not the right firm for you.

01

A startup is not a small corporate.

A corporate reorganises every five years. You reinvent every six months. A structure engineered for stability was never built to survive that rate of change — and neither was the hiring process bolted to it.

02

Build capabilities, not departments.

You may need the capability of a CFO. You rarely need the department. The stage decides how you access it, at what commitment, and for how long — full time is one answer out of eight.

03

Every stage runs a different code.

What won you seed will quietly break your Series B. The expensive mistake is rarely the wrong person; it is running the last stage's playbook one stage too long.

02  /  Services

Founder Code.Leadership.Engine.

Three things a company needs from its people, in the order it usually needs them: the founding combination, the leaders who come after it, and the engine that keeps both supplied. Under each sits the same thread — your stage decides which capability you need next, and how you should access it. The bar under every service shows where it starts earning its keep, from the first co-founder conversation to the board table.

Founder Code

Form the founding team, then keep reading it honestly as the company changes shape. The people decisions you make before employee number one set the ceiling on everything after.

Founder Quotient

Structured assessment of founders and leadership teams.

A BPS-certified read on the people, not the pitch: track record, drive, coachability, judgement under pressure, and how strength and blind spots are distributed across the team. Investors use it before a term sheet. Boards use it before a promotion. Founders use it on themselves, before they scale a company around habits that will not survive the next stage. You get a written assessment and a working conversation, not a score in a portal.

Every stage

Founder Hunter

Co-founder and founding team matchmaking.

We run co-founder search the way we run executive search: a mapped market, structured outreach to people who are not looking, and assessment against the specific gap in the founding combination rather than a generic ideal. We test for complementarity, equity mindset, appetite for the stage you are actually at, and how each of you behaves when things go badly — because that is when founding teams break, not when they are winning. You choose with evidence, not chemistry alone.

Pre-seed → Seed

Founder Inner Circle

A capped, confidential circle of founders and CEOs.

The higher you go, the less honest the feedback gets. A deliberately small group of founders and CEOs roughly one stage apart, meeting under Chatham House rules, where the questions you cannot put to your board or your team get asked properly. Introductions are made because they should be, not because someone is selling. No stage, no sponsors, no content calendar — the value is that the room stays small.

Every stage

Leadership

Staff the C-suite, permanently or on demand. The same market map sits underneath both; what changes is the commitment your stage can carry.

Executive Search

Permanent C-suite and senior leadership search.

Retained search built for startups rather than corporates. We map the market, approach leaders who are not applying anywhere, and assess for founder fit and stage fit as hard as for functional skill — because the CFO who is right for Series A is often wrong at Series C, and the reverse is just as true. We build the compensation case including equity, which is where most regional processes come apart, run it to a signed offer, and stay involved through the first ninety days. Twenty years of GCC search sits behind it.

Series A onward

Executive On-Demand

Fractional and interim executives, scoped to outcomes.

Senior capability without the full-time cost, commitment or risk. Fractional for the leader you need two days a week and cannot yet justify five. Interim for a gap you need covered now — a departure, a transformation, a fundraise. Scoped around what the company actually needs this quarter, with an agreed definition of done and a clean path to convert to permanent or to exit. Frequently the right answer for someone who arrived certain they wanted a full-time hire.

Seed onward

Engine

The hiring and technology engine under the hood. Capacity you can turn up and down with the curve, instead of carrying it permanently and hoping the curve agrees.

TA Powerhouse

Talent acquisition as a service.

Embedded hiring capacity that flexes with your hiring curve instead of against it: a dedicated recruiter or pod working inside your business, on your tools and your cadence, managed by us. It comes with the parts most startups never get round to building — a process that actually closes, structured interviews, calibrated scorecards, compensation benchmarks and an employer story people respond to. It typically pays for itself against agency fees after roughly three hires, and the process stays behind when you scale it back.

Seed onward

Tech Grid

Technology capability, from full time to offshore.

Product, engineering and AI capability accessed in whatever shape the stage can carry: full-time hires, offshore squads, technology as a service, or specialist solution partners for a defined build. Early on it means shipping without standing up a department you cannot yet feed. Later it means adding a capability the internal team does not have, without waiting out a twelve-month hiring cycle. We assemble it and stay accountable for it.
