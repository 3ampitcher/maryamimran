About the club

Small changes, made at campus scale.

The UBT Sustainability Club brings students together around one simple idea: the habits and systems on a university campus are worth fixing, and students are the ones best placed to fix them.

We run awareness campaigns, sorting and recycling initiatives, hands-on workshops and community projects across the year. No environmental background is required — just an interest in doing something useful with your time at UBT.

  • 🌱

    Awareness

    Campaigns and events that make sustainability visible on campus.

  • ♻️

    Action

    Recycling drives, clean-ups and projects with a measurable result.

  • 🤝

    Community

    A network of students, faculty and partners pulling the same direction.

What we do

Six things the club works on.

📣

Campus campaigns

Awareness weeks, stands and student-led pushes around waste, water and energy.

🗂️

Recycling & sorting

Getting bins, signage and sorting habits right in the places students actually use.

🎓

Workshops & talks

Short, practical sessions — upcycling, energy, careers in sustainability.

🧹

Clean-up drives

On-campus and community clean-ups, organised and run by members.

💡

Green projects

Student proposals for saving energy, water and waste across the university.

🌍

Partnerships

Working with other clubs, departments and organisations beyond UBT.
