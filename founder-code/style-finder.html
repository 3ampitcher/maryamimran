Founder Style Finder

Twenty-four statements. Three axes. One founder style.

A free, indicative instrument that places you on the same three preference axes the Founder Quotient assessment uses to derive an archetype — and tells you which of the eight founder styles you most resemble, what it costs you, and who complements you.

Time

About four minutes. Twenty-four agree / disagree statements, one at a time.

Result

Your position on Horizon, Arena and Mode, and the founder style those three produce.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and no account is required.

This is not the Founder Quotient

The Style Finder reports a preference, not an ability, and it produces no score. FQ requires a CV, a pitch deck, evidence of prior outcomes, structured and situational items, an interview, expert assessor observation and follow-up verification. A four-minute self-report cannot substitute for any of that, and this one does not pretend to.

Read it as a starting hypothesis about how you prefer to operate — useful for thinking about co-founders and team design, not for making a hiring or investment decision.

How to answer

Answer for how you actually operate, not how you believe a founder should operate. There are no better or worse answers on any axis — every pole has a benefit and a cost, and the instrument is only useful if it reflects you.

What this result is

An indicative read on operating preference, from self-report alone. It is not a Founder Quotient score, not an assessment of your potential, and not evidence of anything you have built. Style contributes no points to FQ.

If it is useful, it is useful as a conversation: with a co-founder about how you will divide decisions, or with yourself about which seat you should actually be holding.
