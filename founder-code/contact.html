What usually happens next

  1. A scoping conversation. What is being decided, by when, and on what evidence you have now.
  2. A defined scope. Which lenses matter for this decision, which inputs are available, and what confidence the evidence will support.
  3. Assessment. Materials, structured items, interview, verification and expert assessor review.
  4. The dashboard. A single-page Executive Intelligence Dashboard, with the supporting detail behind it and a debrief.

For founders

If you are a founder assessing yourself rather than being assessed by someone else, say so. The debrief is different, the emphasis is on development and co-founder specification, and the profile belongs to you.
