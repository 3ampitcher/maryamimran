The intelligence layer for founder decisions

IQ measures how you think.
EQ measures how you feel.
Neither measures how you build.

Founder selection is the highest-stakes judgement in venture, and it is usually made from a CV, a deck, a warm introduction and an hour of conversation. Founder Code is the missing layer underneath that decision.

Founder Quotient+Founder Hunter=Founder Code

Assess how a founder thinks, operates, leads, builds and fits  ·  then search, match and deploy on that intelligence.

The problem

IQ

Cognitive intelligence

How quickly and how well a person reasons. Well validated — and silent on everything a venture actually demands.

EQ

Emotional intelligence

How a person reads and regulates emotion. Necessary for leadership. Not sufficient for founding.

FQ

Founder Quotient

How a founder thinks, operates, leads, builds and creates value — and how that fits a role, a venture, a stage and a founding team.

Who uses it

Founders & co-founders

Understand yourself, name your edge, and specify the co-founder you actually need.

Investors & VC

Diligence the founder as rigorously as the market, with a targeted verification list.

Studios & venture builders

Match a founder to the venture, the stage and the team you are staffing.

Accelerators & incubators

Select cohorts on structure, then spend mentoring hours where they pay.

Innovation & government

Make founder decisions at volume, comparably and defensibly.

The value

The right founder+the right co-founder or team+the right venture

Founder Code exists to align those three. Every search and assessment also strengthens a proprietary MENAP founder map — more founder intelligence, better matching, stronger data, better decisions.
