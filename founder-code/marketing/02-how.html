02 / 03 How it works

Evidence in. A blueprint out.

A questionnaire alone would be worthless, so it is one input among many. Self-report says how a founder sees themselves; evidence says what they have actually done; the interview says what happens when the two are held against each other.

Step one  —  Assessment inputs

Founder materials

  • Founder CV
  • Pitch deck
  • Relevant founder and venture information
  • Evidence of prior experience, achievements and outcomes

Structured assessment

  • Structured personality questionnaire
  • Structured questions and answers
  • Open-ended questions
  • Situational questions
  • Agree / disagree items

Interview & verification

  • Founder interview
  • Expert assessor observations
  • Follow-up questions and evidence checks
  • Response to challenge
  • Clarification of personal contribution

Step two  —  The three-part score

60% Founder Potential Eight mechanisms  ·  e.g. 82/100
+
20% Founder Experience Evidence & attribution  ·  e.g. 72/100
+
20% Founder–Venture Fit Proximity & insight  ·  e.g. 85/100
=
81 FQ / 100

60% × 82  +  20% × 72  +  20% × 85  =  81

Only three components create the score. Personality explains behaviour and operating preference — it contributes no points. Confidence in the evidence is reported alongside the number.
