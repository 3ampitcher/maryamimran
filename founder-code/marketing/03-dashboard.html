The Executive Intelligence Dashboard is the deliverable: a single visual page that answers six questions without anyone having to read a forty-page report.

Three weighted components, with the arithmetic on the page.

02   What the evidence proves

Experience evidence and confidence level, stated rather than implied.

03   Who the founder is

Archetype, personality signature, strengths, edges and risks.

04   How to deploy them

Leadership readiness, role-fit hypotheses, venture and stage fit.

05   What to diligence

The specific claims requiring verification before a decision.

06   Who to add

Co-founder requirements, team blueprint, primary team risk.
