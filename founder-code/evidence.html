Evidence and credibility

A serious claim about a founder has to be able to say where it came from.

Founder Code combines three things: an independent published research base, two decades of regional founder and C-suite assessment practice, and a controlled methodology that keeps the assessment consistent from one founder to the next.

The mechanisms Founder Quotient assesses are drawn from published, peer-reviewed work on entrepreneurial behaviour, judgement under uncertainty, personality and firm outcomes — research carried out independently, by people with no relationship to Founder Code and no interest in its conclusions.

Why independence matters here

An assessment vendor that funds its own validating research is marking its own homework. Founder Code's research base predates the product and was produced for entirely unrelated purposes. That constrains what we can claim — the research supports the mechanisms, not the instrument — and the methodological position below is written accordingly.

03 — Assessment governance

The same founder, assessed twice, should not get two different answers.

Consistency is a governance problem before it is a statistical one. The Founder Code methodology is controlled and version-locked, so that a profile written this quarter can be compared with one written last year.

  • Seven controlled methodology documents governing the assessment system end to end.
  • Version-locked assessment items — an item cannot change silently between founders.
  • Version-locked scoring rules — weightings and thresholds are fixed to a version.
  • Controlled assessment logic — how evidence maps to a conclusion is written down, not improvised.
  • Consistent documentation of the system, so a client can be told exactly which version produced their profile.

What this buys a client

Comparability across a pipeline, a portfolio or a cohort. Two founders assessed under the same version have been asked the same questions, scored on the same rules and reported against the same thresholds.

It also makes the assessment auditable. When a conclusion is challenged, the evidence, the item and the rule that produced it can all be pointed at.

04 — Methodological position

Founder Code is an evidence-informed assessment under validation.

That sentence is the most important one on this website, and it is deliberately placed where nobody has to look for it. What follows is what the outputs are, and what they are not.

Its outputs are

  • Structured hypotheses for expert verification.
  • Decision-support intelligence.
  • A framework for targeted diligence.
  • A guide for interviews, team design and founder development.

Its outputs are not

  • Deterministic facts.
  • A psychological diagnosis.
  • A pass-or-fail judgement.
  • A replacement for expert assessment.
  • A replacement for commercial, financial, legal or investment diligence.

Why we say it this plainly

Assessment is a field with a long history of instruments that promised more than they could carry. The honest position is that Founder Code's mechanisms are grounded in independent research, its practice is grounded in 700+ direct assessments, and the instrument itself is still being validated. Anyone selling you certainty about a founder is selling you something else.

How that shapes the deliverable

Every profile carries an evidence-confidence level, names the specific claims that still require verification, and ends in a recommendation about where to spend the next hours of diligence — rather than in a verdict. The dashboard is built to be argued with.
