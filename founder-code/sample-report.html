How to read this

The recommendation is a structured hypothesis for expert verification, not a verdict. It tells a decision-maker where to spend the next two hours of diligence — it does not replace commercial, financial, legal or investment diligence.

Reading the dashboard

Designed so the number is never the whole answer.

  • The score is narrow on purpose. Three weighted components, with the arithmetic printed on the page.
  • Confidence sits next to the score. Moderate evidence and strong evidence produce different decisions at the same number.
  • Watch areas are marked, not buried. The lowest mechanism is shown at the same size as the highest.
  • Style carries no points. It appears because it predicts behaviour in a team, not because it earns credit.
  • Every profile ends in an action. What to verify, who to add, what could derail it.

Methodological position

Founder Code is an evidence-informed assessment under validation. Its outputs are structured hypotheses for expert verification, decision-support intelligence and a framework for targeted diligence — not deterministic facts, not a psychological diagnosis, not a pass-or-fail judgement, and not a replacement for expert assessment or for commercial, financial, legal or investment diligence.
